# Current Process — Discovery Baseline

This document records the starting understanding and must be validated with residents/administration.

## Pilot building

- 5 floors.
- 2 units per floor.
- 10 units total: 1A–5B.

A unit may eventually have more than one resident account; the system must not equate "10 units" with "10 users".

## Booking concepts discussed

### Leisure usage

- Short usage, tentatively one or two hours.
- SUM only.
- Can be shared or exclusive.
- Shared reservations may coexist if users accept shared usage.
- Exclusive leisure would have a higher fee than shared leisure.

### Events

Tentative time windows:
- afternoon/day slot;
- night slot;
- potentially a combined full-day option later.

SUM is the base resource. Pool and barbecue/grill may be added to qualifying event reservations for additional charges.

### Payment

Two paths are currently envisioned:

1. Mercado Pago.
2. Cash, requiring controlled confirmation by an authorized person/admin.

A booking may remain temporarily held while payment is pending. DEC-015 sets
the pilot hold duration to 24 hours, supplied through configuration.

## Administration

The administrator needs visibility over reservations, payment state, history and exception handling. Administrative changes must be traceable.

## Communication

Messaging is envisioned primarily to coordinate compatible/shared reservations, not as a general building chat.

## Validation needed

All prices, exact event time windows, cleaning policy, cancellation/refund policy, cash receiver/confirmation process, maximum shared capacity and payment deadlines remain subject to stakeholder validation.
